## Invitation (email)

**Subject:** Help us test a new volleyball club app (45 minutes, your phone)

Hi [first name],

We are building 5x1, an app that helps volleyball clubs manage members, teams, training and match RSVPs, and attendance. Before we write any code, we want to check that the screens make sense to the people who would use them.

We would like you to try a clickable prototype on your phone for about 45 minutes, with a short call. You will be asked to do a few everyday tasks, such as answering whether you are coming to training.

- The prototype uses sample data. Nothing you enter is saved, and sign-in is not real.
- We are testing the app, not you. There are no right or wrong answers, and you can stop at any time.
- Please read the consent notice below before the session.

## Consent notice (for participants)

**What this is:** a usability test of a prototype of the 5x1 app, run by [Owner name] for [organisation / project owner] on [date].

**What we ask you to do:** complete about eleven short tasks on the prototype, then answer ten short questions about how easy it was. The session takes about 45 minutes.

**What we record:** notes on whether each task was completed, how long it took, and short quotes from what you say. We do not record video or audio unless you agree separately. We use a code (P1 to P5) in our notes, not your name.

**Who sees it:** only [Owner name] and the project team. Findings are reported without names.

**How long we keep it:** notes are deleted after the phase 2 gate in the project plan, or earlier if you ask us to.

**Your choices:** taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. You can ask to see or remove your notes. You can contact [contact email] with any question.

**Who can take part:** adults only (18 or older). If you are under 18, please tell us and we will not run the session.
